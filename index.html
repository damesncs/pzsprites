pzsprites

pzsprites is a no-frills library for creating 2D games with physics simulation provided by planck.js.

Demos

  • Ball Toss - simple physics and collisions demonstration - click to create a ball, click and drag on balls to throw them
  • Truck - driving demo, arrow keys to drive
  • Plane - simple flight simulation with advanced physics, press W to apply thrust, up/down arrows for elevator
  • Volley Ball - a simple physics game similar to volley ball
